The Cold Place

The Cold Place is an original five-page comic created for the December 2025 issue of BAZOOF! Magazine. The story explores loneliness, friendship, and the experience of being different. Written for readers ages 10–12, the comic combines fantasy with familiar emotions, encouraging empathy and acceptance. I developed the story and illustrations from the initial concept through to the final print-ready publication.

Visual Storytelling Working in a short comic format meant finding ways to communicate the characters' personalities and emotions without relying too heavily on dialogue. Expressions, gestures, and composition became important storytelling tools.

Inspiration I wanted to capture the particular feeling of childhood winter stories, where the natural world seems mysterious and alive, and the line between reality and folklore becomes blurred.
